# TACO — Personas & Jobs To Be Done
> Two core personas, their primary jobs, success metrics, and the gap between today's manual process and TACO's automated insights

**Author:** Victoria Marchant — Insights & Analytics
**Date:** October 2025
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/IA/pages/40303690158

---

## 🎯 Context

TACO's Person Insights feature is designed around two validated personas: Line Managers preparing for 1:1s and P&C Advisors supporting leaders with consistent reporting.
Each persona has a primary Job To Be Done, defined success metrics, and a clear before/after workflow.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Managers manually extract data from OpenAir, upload to ad-hoc tools, and spend 20–30 minutes interpreting results per person.
P&C advisors collect fragmented exports from multiple managers with no consistency, no audit trail, and no standard format.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Persona: Line Manager (People Leader)**
Time-poor leader preparing weekly 1:1s and monthly reviews.
Needs a fast, trustworthy view of what each person did and where risks are.
KPIs: team utilisation, billable capture, timesheet compliance, data quality.
Goals: high-quality 1:1 prep in under 2 minutes per person.

**2. Line Manager — Job To Be Done**
"As a people leader, when I prepare for a 1:1, I want a concise, trustworthy last-month summary of what this person worked on so I can coach effectively, rebalance workload, and ensure accurate T&M capture."
Motivation: fairness, performance, compliance, margin protection.
Frequency: weekly. Importance: high. Segment: all people leaders.

**3. Line Manager — current process vs desired**
Today: manual extract from OpenAir → upload to AI tool → interpret results → create talking points.
With TACO: select person → auto 30-day insights and anomalies → saved reports + natural language query → export with audit trail → flag entries for reprocessing.

**4. Line Manager — success metrics**
Prep time per 1:1: target under 2 minutes.
Actionable insights per person: tracked per review cycle.
Improved timesheeting accuracy: trending down on Unknowns and misclassifications.
Fewer escalations. Greater visibility. Increased adoption of common reports.

**5. Persona: People & Culture (P&C) Advisor**
Partner to leaders — needs consistent, auditable person-level reporting.
Responsible for coaching, compliance, wellbeing signals, and resource utilisation.
KPIs: timesheet compliance, equitable workload, policy adherence.
Goals: standardise reporting, identify wellbeing risks, surface work patterns.

**6. P&C Advisor — Job To Be Done**
"As a P&C advisor, when supporting leaders, I want consistent, auditable person-level reports showing what people actually worked on so I can advise on performance, development, wellbeing, and policy adherence with evidence."
Motivation: fairness, compliance, consistent employee experience.
Frequency: monthly/quarterly and on-demand. Segment: all P&C advisors.

**7. P&C Advisor — current process vs desired**
Today: collect fragmented exports → normalise into narrative → cross-reference billing → follow up for clarifications.
With TACO: governed access to standardised "last 30 days" person pack → common reports → exportable evidence with audit trail → coaching narratives → flagging for reprocessing.

**8. P&C Advisor — success metrics**
% of leaders using standardised person reports for 1:1s and reviews.
Trend reduction in Unknowns and low-quality descriptions.
Improved ability to discuss performance patterns with objective data.
Cleaner audits. Earlier detection of wellbeing risks.

**9. Shared success indicators**
Leading: adoption of common reports, median prep time, data coverage, tool satisfaction.
Lagging: reduction in timesheeting anomalies, zero unauthorised access incidents.
Both personas benefit from the same platform — different views, same governed data.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Design TACO's interface to serve both personas from a single platform — with role-appropriate views, allow-list governance, and shared common reports.

---

## 🔑 Close

> Two personas. One platform. Same data. Different views. Governed access.

Line Managers get fast 1:1 prep. P&C Advisors get consistent, auditable reporting. Both get evidence-based conversations.
