# Conver — Marketing Guide
> What to say, how to say it, and what assets to use when presenting Conver to 6,500 employees

**Author:** Ben — UX Design Intern
**Date:** March 2026
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/IA

---

## 🎯 Context

Conver needs a product page that communicates its value to every employee — not just technical users.
The page must answer three questions immediately: What does it do? Is it safe? How do I start?

---

## 🔍 Problem

The biggest risk is leading with developer language that alienates 90% of the audience.
Don't lead with "Ship great agents, fast." Don't use LLM, MCP, inference in the hero.
Don't list limitations upfront. Don't stuff every feature into the hero section.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Trust-first hero — one message, commit to it**
Recommended: "AI that works the way Datacom does."
Alternative: "The AI platform built for everyone at Datacom."
Alternative: "Work smarter. Stay secure. Use Conver."
Subheadline: access to powerful AI models, custom agents, and automation tools — hosted in Australia.

**2. Trust bar — credibility in 5 seconds**
6,500 employees | Hosted in Australia | Built by Datacom | Zero data used for training.
All verified stats from product documentation.
Appears immediately below the hero. No explanation needed.

**3. Three core feature cards**
Card 1 — Chat with powerful AI models. GPT-5.1, Claude Sonnet, Gemini Flash.
Card 2 — Build and run custom agents. Tailored to role, team, workflow.
Card 3 — Analyse files and search the web. PDFs, Word, Excel, images. Bing Search with citations.

**4. Getting started — 3 steps, remove all friction**
Request access via Activate.
Complete compliance training (~2 weeks).
Log in at conver.datacom.com with SSO. No new password.
Video: "Your First Conversation."

**5. Security section — answer "is this safe?" head-on**
Hosted in Sydney (Azure East). Inference stays in-region.
Conversations never shared or used for training. Private like email.
Full pen test completed. SSO + network controls + continuous monitoring.
DLP guardrails block PII, passwords, and sensitive data.

**6. AI model showcase — three tiers**
Advanced & Powerful: GPT-5.1, Claude Sonnet 4.5, o3.
Balanced Performance: GPT-4.1, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Claude 3.7 Sonnet.
Quick & Simple: GPT-5 Mini, GPT-5 Nano, Claude Haiku.
Visual assets: model category SVGs and individual model PNGs.

**7. Agent demo — the most impressive capability**
"Build once, automate forever."
Create a custom agent in minutes. Give it instructions. Connect tools. Let it handle the rest.
Videos: Building an Agent (primary), Agent Chaining, Bookmarks, Renaming/Archiving.

**8. One-liners for every context**
Hero: "AI that works the way Datacom does."
Email subject: "Conver is live — AI built for everyone at Datacom."
Slack: "Think ChatGPT, but built for Datacom. Secure. Local. Yours."
Tooltip: "Conver — your secure AI assistant, hosted in Australia."
Short pitch: "Multiple AI models. Custom agents. Your data never leaves Datacom."

**9. What NOT to lead with**
"Ship great agents, fast" — developer language, excludes everyone.
Technical terms in hero copy — LLM, MCP, inference, Azure Bedrock.
Limitations listed upfront — kills momentum before value is established.
Every feature in the hero — pick ONE message.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Lead with trust. End with CTAs. Primary: "Launch Conver" at conver.datacom.com. Secondary: "Get a demo" at conver@datacom.com.

---

## 🔑 Close

> Make every employee believe AI is for them. Establish absolute trust. Remove every barrier between "I'm curious" and "I'm using it."

Trust bar: 6,500 employees | Hosted in Australia | Built by Datacom | Zero data used for training.
