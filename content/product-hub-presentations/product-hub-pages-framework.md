## 🎯 Context

Four products need pages in the Product Hub: Conver, TACO, Agent Library, and Discovery Engine.
Every page uses the same mandatory 6-section framework.
Goal: a reader understands the product and is ready to use it in under 15 minutes — no meeting required.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Without a standardised framework, product pages are inconsistent.
Some are too technical. Others lack practical detail. Most fail to convert a curious visitor into an active user.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. The 6-section framework**
Section 1 — What It Is (150 words). Plain English, no jargon.
Section 2 — Who It's For (200 words). Specific roles, not departments.
Section 3 — Why It Matters (300 words). Problem + value levers: time, cost, risk.
Section 4 — How to Use It (500 words). Step-by-step, 3+ steps, screenshots.
Section 5 — Examples (500 words). 2+ real use cases with input and output.
Section 6 — Run It (100 words). Live URL or Docker command.
