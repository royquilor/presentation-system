# Discovery Workshops Feb 2026
> Resolver Group Discovery Workshops outcomes and next steps

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 16 February 2026
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40777285831

---

## 🎯 Context

Resolver Group Discovery Workshops were held in February 2026 and attended by 42 Resolver Team Leads and Members. The workshops established current-state baseline for people, process, and tools to inform future-state CSDM structure, access design, and transition planning.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Understanding current-state resolver group structure, process usage, and tool integrations is essential before designing future-state ServiceNow configuration. Without discovery, migration risks misalignment, incorrect assignment groups, and integration gaps.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. People—current-state baseline established**
Resolver group memberships validated, including identification of inactive or missing groups. Teams classified as Datacom-only or leveraged, informing future assignment group and access design. Provides reliable baseline for team-level change impact and ServiceNow set-up.

**2. Process usage clarified**
Teams confirmed which ITIL processes they actively use today: incident, change, problem, request, knowledge, asset, CMDB. Knowledge is distributed across multiple platforms (Confluence, SharePoint, Unity). Locations captured to support future ServiceNow knowledge integration and self-service.

**3. Documentation locations**
Existing documentation locations identified, highlighting variation and standardisation opportunities. Supports future knowledge integration planning.

**4. Tools and integrations**
Known and potential future ServiceNow integration needs identified. Tools currently used alongside Cherwell documented.

**5. Workshop attendance**
42 Resolver Team Leads and Members attended. Feedback captured in Resolver Group Workshop Feedback.xlsx.

**6. Whiteboard and feedback**
Whiteboard and feedback spreadsheet available for reference. Links to Confluence whiteboard and SharePoint feedback document.

**7. Key outcome—baseline for change impact**
Current-state data informs future-state CSDM structure, access design, and potential out-of-tool process updates.

**8. Key outcome—targeted deep dives**
Smaller, process-specific sessions (e.g. change, asset, CMDB) will be scheduled with individual teams, Process Owners, and FSD Business Readiness Team.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Use workshop outputs to inform future-state design and transition planning. Consolidate data, validate gaps, and schedule targeted deep dives.

- Consolidate and validate workshop outputs into structured datasets
- Map current-state to future-state CSDM structure and access
- Schedule process-specific deep dives with Process Owners and FSD Business Readiness
- Use outputs to inform training and communications ahead of go-live

*Discovery establishes a reliable baseline for ServiceNow configuration and change impact assessment.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Data gaps:** Inactive or missing groups may require follow-up validation.
- **Knowledge distribution:** Multiple platforms (Confluence, SharePoint, Unity) complicate consolidation planning.

---

## ✅ Next Steps

1. **Data consolidation** — Consolidate workshop outputs into structured datasets; follow up on gaps with relevant teams.
2. **Future-state mapping** — Map current-state to CSDM structure, access, and out-of-tool process updates.
3. **Targeted deep dives** — Schedule process-specific sessions (change, asset, CMDB) with Process Owners and FSD Business Readiness.
4. **Training and comms** — Use outputs to inform training and communications ahead of go-live.
