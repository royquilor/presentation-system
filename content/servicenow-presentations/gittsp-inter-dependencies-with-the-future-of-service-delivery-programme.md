# Inter-Dependencies with the Future of Service Delivery Programme
> Dependencies between Group IT Transition project and FSD programme

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 20 November 2025
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40354873423

---

## 🎯 Context

The Group IT Transition project operates in parallel with the Future of Service Delivery (FSD) programme, which transitions external customers from Cherwell to ServiceNow. The project depends on FSD for platform capability, resources, process documentation, and data migration support. A dependency register tracks requested items and status.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Without clear dependency management, the project may be blocked by FSD delivery delays or lack of access to shared resources. Platform capability, integrations, InWOW documentation, SME capacity, and data migration support must be coordinated to avoid schedule impact. The project maintains engagement with FSD for interdependencies per the PID.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Platform capability and integrations**
FSD Programme's delivery of Platform capability, including Integrations required for Corporate Group IT—Due: Before Feb 2026. Critical path for project build and go-live.

**2. FSD programme resources**
FSD Programme resources such as SMEs and shared ServiceNow Platform Architect capacity—Ongoing. Greg Burns (Platform Architect) is shared; SME availability may be constrained.

**3. InWOW documentation**
FSD Programme's InWOW documentation relating to all processes that have evolved and will evolve throughout FSD Implementation—Due: Before 21 November 2025. Includes: finalised process designs for as-built Incident, Service Request, Problem, Catalogue Items, Change, Knowledge, CMDB, Maintenance tasks, Interactions, Reporting, Surveys.

**4. InWOW engagement**
FSD's InWOW Engagement in programme's champion/SME network, communications networks, channels and key messaging—Ongoing. Supports change management and adoption.

**5. Data migration and cleansing**
Support with Data Migration & Cleansing, including transformation of source system extracts—Ongoing. As coordinated by FSD Data Migration team.

**6. Professional Services consultancy**
Datacom ServiceNow Professional Services Team Consultancy services—TBC.

**7. Dependency register**
Register of requested items and current status: Output Requests from FSD for Internal Corp Group IT Transition Project.xlsx (SharePoint). Tracks all dependency requests and fulfilment.

**8. Governance alignment**
Project Governance and Steering Committee manage FSD dependencies; Migration Schedule and Change Management are Consulted with FSD per RACI.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Maintain active dependency management with FSD programme through the dependency register and Steering Committee.

- Track platform capability and integration delivery (Before Feb 2026)
- Secure InWOW documentation for process designs
- Coordinate FSD resource availability (Platform Architect, SMEs)
- Engage FSD Data Migration team for cleansing and transformation support
- Resolve Professional Services consultancy scope (TBC)
- Use Output Requests register for status visibility

*Parallel delivery with FSD requires ongoing coordination to avoid schedule and resource conflicts.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Platform capability delay:** If FSD delivery slips past Feb 2026, project build and go-live may be impacted.
- **Resource contention:** Shared Platform Architect and SME capacity may create bottlenecks.
