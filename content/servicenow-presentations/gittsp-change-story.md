# Change Story
> Documenting the agreed summary of change for Group IT Transition to ServiceNow

**Author:** Muskan Verma
**Date:** 22 November 2025
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40419524824

---

## 🎯 Context

This document captures the agreed summary of change for the Group IT Transition to ServiceNow (GITTSP) programme. It supports Stakeholder Assessment and Key Messaging development. The change moves Corporate/Group IT from Cherwell to ServiceNow by March 2026, with the FSD Programme already migrating approximately 40 customers and their teams each month.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Cherwell reaches end-of-life on 31 December 2026. Continuing to run it poses technical, security, and commercial risk. The current environment is fragmented across multiple tenancies with inconsistent RBAC, siloed data, and significant manual work. The ITSM tool's capability lags behind competitors.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. What is changing**
A single governed CMDB and domain-separated architecture; standardised ITIL-aligned workflows for incident, request, change, problem, and knowledge management; modern capabilities including portal-based ticketing, virtual agent, embedded knowledge, automated SLA/OLA tracking, and enhanced reporting; ServiceNow ITOM Discovery replacing multiple inconsistent asset/configuration tracking methods; organisational change from customised siloed processes to standardised automated "future ways of working."

**2. Timeline and scope**
Group IT and teams that interact with Group IT to resolve Cherwell tickets today are transitioning in March 2026. The FSD Programme is already migrating approximately 40 customers and their teams each month.

**3. Implementation approach**
Adopt out-of-the-box ServiceNow configuration wherever possible; perform detailed process and data discovery including tenancy consolidation, CMDB cleanse, and resolver mapping; roll out new FSD processes for incident, problem, change, request, and knowledge management; implement Datacom Managing Instance (DMI) for internal IT; integrate with key tools (Activate, Jitterbit, xMatters, Genesys); provide targeted change management with training and champions.

**4. Benefits for staff**
Self-service capabilities with clear status tracking; fewer manual steps and automated routing and approvals; one source of truth for assets and configuration; role-based workspaces designed for specific tasks.

**5. Benefits for Datacom**
Lower operational costs from reduced ticket volumes and FTE hours; ability to scale services rapidly without added complexity.

**6. Support provided**
Change champions and training with role-based training plans, hands-on labs, and ServiceNow University resources; knowledge base and quick guides with task-focused "how-to" content aligned to new workflows.

**7. Ticketing transformation**
From email-driven ticketing to portal for requests, virtual agent, and integrated knowledge; from manual routing and categorisation to automated CI-driven assignment and SLA tracking.

**8. Knowledge and reporting**
From scattered knowledge articles to single repository with search, tagging, and review workflow; from limited reporting to real-time dashboards, trend analysis, and actionable insights; from reactive resolution focus to proactive problem management and prevention.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Execute the transition with minimal customisation and maximum standardisation. Deliver the Datacom Managing Instance (DMI) for internal IT, connecting to the MSP instance where needed.

- Adopt out-of-the-box ServiceNow configuration wherever possible
- Perform detailed process and data discovery before go-live
- Roll out FSD processes automated where possible
- Provide targeted change management and training
- Integrate with key tools for continuity

*This change sets Corporate/Group IT up as "customer zero" for best practice ServiceNow adoption, influencing how other teams transition.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Cherwell end-of-life:** Technical, security, and commercial risk if transition delays beyond 31 December 2026.
- **Fragmented environment:** Multiple tenancies and inconsistent RBAC create migration complexity and data quality risks.

---

2. **Training and readiness** — Deliver role-based training plans, hands-on labs, and knowledge base content.
3. **Go-live readiness** — Ensure March 2026 transition for Group IT and resolver teams.
