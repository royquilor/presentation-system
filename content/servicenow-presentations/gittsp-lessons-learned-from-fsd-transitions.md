# Lessons Learned from FSD Transitions
> Top 5 themes from FSD customer transitions applicable to Group IT Transition project

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 10 November 2025
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40308670640

---

## 🎯 Context

Lessons from FSD (Future of Service Delivery) customer transitions were consolidated into Top 10 themes, then filtered to Top 5 most applicable to the Group IT Transition project's "Ignite FSD" journey. These lessons inform requirements validation, testing, training, data governance, and hypercare planning to avoid repeat issues.

---

## 🔍 Problem

FSD customer go-lives (e.g., 12-May-25, 11-Jun-25, 4-Aug-25) revealed recurring issues: assumptions on functionality, flawed UAT design, training gaps, data ownership ambiguity, and hypercare visibility. Without applying these lessons, the Group IT transition risks similar post-go-live defects and adoption inefficiencies.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Requirements Validation (High severity, 2 recurrences)**
Assumptions on functionality resulted in issues post-Go-Live; lack of functionality validation found post-production. Recommendation: Formalize validation with Platform Enablement teams to ensure no functionality gaps pre-release. Discovery workshops must remove ambiguity; Change Impact Analysis needs completed simultaneously with Discovery and validated with Business stakeholders.

**2. UAT flawed design (High severity, 2 recurrences)**
UAT lacked robust test cases; PO absence caused defects in production. Recommendation: Include operational staff early; design rigorous UAT test cases. Test Strategy must be produced and distributed to key stakeholders; new team processes need end user experience testing; internal DMI Portal requires UAT from Business End Users.

**3. Training Needs Analysis (High severity, 2 recurrences)**
No collaborative training needs analysis was done; inefficiency in tool use due to lack of training. Recommendation: Form dedicated training working group to analyze and address training needs proactively. Different teams have different complexity; training plan and communications plan must feed delivery plan with resources assigned.

**4. Data Ownership Clarity (High severity, 2 recurrences)**
Lack of clarity on customer data ownership delayed migration planning and execution. Recommendation: Clearly delineate ownership responsibilities and data cleansing requirements; address inclusion criteria early in planning. CIs and Knowledge Base articles must be documented with ownership in Discovery; establish data governance framework post-Discovery.

**5. Hypercare visibility (High severity, 2 recurrences)**
Lack of visibility of hypercare processes and issues; impacted business areas excluded from communications. Recommendation: Include impacted areas in hypercare communications and meetings. Clear hypercare processes with SLAs resolving issues in 2-week period; effective transition to BAU.

**6. Additional themes (excluded from Top 5)**
Capability awareness gaps (Medium); Process change communication (Medium); Team collaboration (Low, positive); Business SME Engagement (Medium, positive); Reporting Efficiency (Medium). Programme Governance and Planning excluded as not part of this project journey.

**7. Positive culture**
Strong team dynamic and mutual support without prompts; collaboration with Process Owners and SMEs improved operational perspectives. Maintain motivation and align programme goals; increase SME engagement.

**8. Delivery approach**
Requirements can be delivered agile with user stories; foster culture of positive collaboration; teams must work together, not in siloes; effort in Discovery sessions focused on improvement.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Apply Top 5 lessons to Group IT Transition planning and execution.

- Formalize requirements validation with Platform Enablement before build
- Design rigorous UAT with operational staff involvement; produce Test Strategy for stakeholders
- Form dedicated training working group; complete Training Needs Analysis proactively
- Document data ownership and cleansing in Discovery; establish data governance framework
- Include impacted areas in hypercare communications; define 2-week SLA for issue resolution

*Applying FSD lessons reduces risk of post-go-live defects, adoption inefficiencies, and delayed migration.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Lesson application gaps:** Lessons may not be fully embedded if not explicitly assigned to workstream owners.
- **Resource constraints:** Training working group and rigorous UAT require dedicated capacity.
