# Project Initiation Document
> Transition Datacom Corporate Group IT functions and support processes onto the ServiceNow platform

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 28 January 2026
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40269513507

---

## 🎯 Context

In October 2024 Datacom's Board approved a Business Case to transition Datacom's service delivery to the ServiceNow platform. In October 2025, the FSD Programme achieved the delivery milestone of "Objective 1: Foundational Customer Service" build of ServiceNow, as required to replace Cherwell. This project transitions Corporate Group IT services and functions of Datacom's Infrastructure Products from Cherwell to ServiceNow while the FSD Programme continues transitioning external customers.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Corporate Group IT and resolver teams currently operate on Cherwell. The project must migrate these functions to ServiceNow, implement ITOM Discovery, apply best practice processes, and ensure organisational change management enables successful adoption—all while maintaining engagement with the Future of Service Delivery programme for interdependencies.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Project scope and objectives**
The project will transition Datacom Corporate / Group IT functions, support processes, and resolver teams from Cherwell to ServiceNow; implement ServiceNow ITOM Discovery for Datacom internal; apply ServiceNow best practice processes and platform configuration patterns; ensure OCM activities enable adoption; and maintain parallel delivery with FSD programme interdependencies.

**2. Out of scope**
Migration of external customer workloads (managed within FSD); onboarding of teams not currently operating out of Cherwell; delivery of integrations unrelated to Corporate Group IT operations—unless agreed through Governance.

**3. Milestone timeline**
Initiation Phase closed 31/10/2025 (2 weeks); Discovery Phase closed 5/12/2025 (6 weeks); Planning Post-Discovery in progress 19/12/2025 (2 weeks); Delivery and Implementation Phase 12/01/2026; Transition Complete 01/05/2026; Close Out 29/05/2026.

Enhanced operational efficiency through ServiceNow capabilities to streamline manual work and reduce FTEs; improved customer service for more responsive interactions; strategic alignment with customers who use ServiceNow and future market expectations.

## 💡 Proposal

Execute the transition of Corporate Group IT from Cherwell to ServiceNow following the agreed milestones, applying ServiceNow best practice where beneficial and adapting only as needed for operational requirements.

- Transition resolver groups and support processes identified in Discovery
- Implement ITOM Discovery for Datacom internal
- Apply ITIL-aligned workflows and platform configuration patterns
- Enable OCM for processes, workflows, and integrations
- Maintain FSD programme engagement for interdependencies

*The project delivers internal IT onto the same platform as customer-facing services, enabling operational consistency and strategic alignment.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **FSD dependency:** Parallel delivery with FSD programme requires ongoing coordination; schedule changes may impact milestones.
- **Scope creep:** Out-of-scope items (external workloads, new teams, unrelated integrations) must be governed through formal variation requests.
