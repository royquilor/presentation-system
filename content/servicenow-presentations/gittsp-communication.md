# Communication
> Communication strategy for Elevation Control LAR and application control initiatives

**Author:** Muskan Verma
**Date:** 20 November 2025
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40419098945

---

## 🎯 Context

This document outlines the communication approach for the Elevation Control LAR initiative, which is part of the Application Control initiative. It covers communications for all employees and business units through the awareness, desire, knowledge, and reinforcement stages.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Elevation Control LAR requires clear communication to ensure employees understand what it is, what actions they must take, and when. Without proper awareness and understanding, deployment could face resistance, confusion, or support issues.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Initial awareness**
Databytes and Elevation Control LAR communications target all employees. Internal Comms team delivers email 2 weeks prior. Each group/business unit receives awareness through Organisation Change Agent.

**2. Early stage understanding**
Business Units receive "Elevation Control is coming" messaging. Organisation Change Agent delivers Teams Live Session for knowledge. Each Business Unit receives information about Elevation Control, what it is, and what action users must take.

**3. Pre-enablement reminder**
Reminder communications pre-deployment target all employees. Application Control Inbox delivers email 1 week prior. Motivation phase for desire.

**4. Post-enablement reinforcement**
Email 1 week post deployment. Feedback messaging: "Your feedback is essential to ensure we address any issues and optimize the deployment of all users." All employees targeted.

**5. Feedback collection**
MS Feedback forms and MS Forms surveys gather feedback from all Business Units. MS Forms help gather feedback/insights. Post-deployment feedback via Application Control inbox.

**6. Communication cadence**
Email 2 weeks prior (initial awareness); Teams Live Session (knowledge); Email 1 week prior (reminder); Email 1 week post (reinforcement and feedback).

**7. Ownership**
Internal Comms team, Organisation Change Agent, and Application Control Inbox handle different stages. Business Unit-level communication ensures targeted reach.

**8. Channels**
Email, Teams Live Session, MS Feedback forms, MS Forms surveys. Channels align with ADKAR model (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Reinforcement).

---

## 💡 Proposal

Execute a phased communication plan aligned with the ADKAR change model for Elevation Control LAR deployment.

- Deliver initial awareness 2 weeks prior via email
- Conduct Teams Live Sessions for knowledge transfer
- Send reminder 1 week prior to deployment
- Reinforce and collect feedback 1 week post deployment

*Structured communication ensures employees understand Elevation Control and take required actions.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Timing:** Short lead times (1–2 weeks) may not allow sufficient absorption for all business units.
- **Feedback loop:** Reliance on MS Forms may yield incomplete engagement if not promoted effectively.

---

## ✅ Next Steps

1. **Schedule communications** — Confirm dates for 2-week prior, 1-week prior, and 1-week post emails.
2. **Teams Live Sessions** — Coordinate with Organisation Change Agent across business units.
3. **Feedback analysis** — Review MS Forms responses and address issues post-deployment.

---
