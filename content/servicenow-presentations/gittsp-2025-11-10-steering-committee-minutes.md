# 2025-11-10 Steering Committee Minutes
> Group IT Transition to ServiceNow – Project initiation and discovery phase kick-off

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 10 November 2025
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40354152449

---

## Context

Steering committee meeting for the Group IT Transition to ServiceNow (GITTSP) project. Present: Julian Buckley, Jason Chau, Clinton Cooper, Jessie Gisler, Sumindre Gunawardane, Jonathan Lim, Shawn Sutton, Joe Thornley. Absent: Josh Jacobs. The meeting covered project initiation approval, discovery phase updates, and governance for the Cherwell-to-ServiceNow migration.

---

## Problem

The committee needed to approve the Project Initiation Document (PID) and align on scope, including whether internal ITOM Discovery rollout should be explicitly included. The team also needed to establish discovery phase priorities and address emerging transformation opportunities while ensuring alignment with FSD processes and associated projects.

---

## Observations

**1. Project Updates and Status Format**
Jessie presented the project update format including progress against milestones, risks, issues, and resource status, with regular status reports in Confluence. The team noted the benefits of using Confluence for documentation and tracking. Jessie confirmed no major risks, issues, or resource constraints currently, but these would be built out for the next meeting. Shawn and Julian discussed the need for clear resource planning and visibility, including linking to JPD for shared resource management.

**2. Project Initiation Document Approval**
The Project Initiation Document (PID) incorporated feedback to align with FSD processes. Scope includes parallel delivery with FSD, leveraging best practices, and prioritising foundational data (CMDB, service catalogue). Jessie agreed to explicitly include internal ITOM Discovery rollout in the scope, as discussed with Shawn. The document was agreed by Sponsors and Steering Committee, pending addition of ITOM.

**3. Discovery Phase Overview**
Jessie provided an overview of the discovery phase: Confluence setup, scope definition, and early analysis of transformation opportunities. Jonathan detailed categorisation of resolver groups for ServiceNow transition and ongoing user persona analysis. Early transformation opportunities identified include enhancing CMDB and asset management, improving compliance, and leveraging underused ServiceNow modules.

**4. Shawn Sutton’s Additional Recommendations**
Shawn suggested evaluating the ServiceNow mobile app and major incident management module for internal use. He highlighted interplay with other projects (AI OPS, Red Hat automation) as important for alignment and avoiding delays. Naming standards for groups and services in ServiceNow were proposed to improve consistency.

**5. Business Prioritisation Board Presentation**
Jessie mentioned the upcoming presentation to the Business Prioritisation Board to ensure project visibility and alignment. The team agreed to bring recommendations and decisions on user onboarding and resolver group inclusion to future meetings.

---

## Proposal

- **Project Initiation Document Agreed** — by Sponsors and Steering Committee (pending addition of ITOM).
- Add internal ITOM Discovery rollout explicitly to high-level project scope.
- Bring user onboarding and resolver group recommendations to future meetings.

---

## Risks

- **Scope alignment:** Need to ensure ITOM, service catalogue, and associated projects (AI OPS, Red Hat) are clearly documented and aligned to avoid confusion and delays.
- **Resource visibility:** Clear resource planning and JPD linkage required for shared resource management.

---

## Close

The committee endorsed the PID and discovery approach. Internal ITOM Discovery, service catalogue definition, and alignment with associated projects were confirmed as priorities for Jessie to action before the next meeting.
