# Transition Plan - Group Tech & EAS to ServiceNow
> 16-week outlook for Datacom Group Tech and EAS transition from Cherwell to ServiceNow (February – May 2026)

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 18 February 2026
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40675508248

---

## 🎯 Context

The Transition Plan outlines the high-level phasing and 16-week delivery outlook for moving Datacom Corporate Group Tech and EAS from Cherwell to ServiceNow. Initiation and Discovery phases are complete; the project is in Delivery and Implementation Phase with Transition Complete targeted for May 2026 and Close Out 29 May 2026.

---

## 🔍 Problem

The transition requires coordinated delivery across multiple capabilities—Portal, CMDB, ITSM processes, ITOM, Knowledge Management, Reporting—while aligning with FSD programme patterns and maintaining operational continuity. A clear plan with milestones, RACI, and capability streams is essential to manage complexity and dependencies.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. High-level phasing**
Initiation Phase closed 31/10/2025 (2 weeks); Discovery Phase closed 5/12/2025 (6 weeks); Planning Post-Discovery in progress; Delivery and Implementation Phase started 12/01/2026; Transition Complete targeted May 2026; Close Out 29/05/2026.

**2. Capabilities and processes**
Portal Usage (portal-first logging, self-help); Catalogue Management; CMDB Management; ITSM Incident, Request Fulfilment, Problem, Maintenance Planning, Change, Major Incident Management; Asset Management; Event Management / ITOM; Knowledge Management; Service Catalogue / Offerings; Reporting & Analytics; Integration.

**3. RACI for MSP transition**
Defined RACI for MSP transition activities to clarify accountability across FSD and Group IT project teams.

**4. 16-week outlook**
February–May 2026 timeline with detailed workstreams; visual timeline available in SharePoint (Project Delivery Timeline – Corporate IT EAS Move to ServiceNow).

**5. DRAFT streams of focus**
Workstreams defined for parallel delivery; alignment with CSDM, Zero Touch Service Desk, and Discovery outputs.

**6. Alignment with FSD**
Transition plan follows FSD programme patterns and leverages DMI platform capability delivered by FSD.

**7. Go-live target**
Transition Complete milestone set for May 2026, with Close Out 29 May 2026.

**8. Capability rollout**
Phased rollout of portal, CMDB, automation, and ITSM processes per business case and solution design.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Execute the 16-week transition plan following ServiceNow best practices and FSD alignment.

- Progress Delivery and Implementation Phase through defined workstreams
- Implement capabilities in sequence: Portal, CMDB, ITSM processes, ITOM, Knowledge, Reporting
- Maintain RACI clarity for MSP transition and internal handover
- Target Transition Complete May 2026; Close Out 29 May 2026

*The plan provides a structured path from current state to ServiceNow go-live with clear milestones and capability streams.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Resource availability:** Key SMEs and platform team capacity may constrain parallel workstream delivery.
- **FSD programme alignment:** Dependencies on FSD platform capability and integrations may affect milestones.
