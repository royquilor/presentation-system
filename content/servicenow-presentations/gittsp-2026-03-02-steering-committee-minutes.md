## Observations

**1. Action Updates**
Mark Smith, SACM, FSD CMDB alignment meetings booked for week commencing 2 March; very little availability from POs. Approach to Application-based Services Modelling circulated. Zero Touch: Jessie now working with Jai Moran (previously Persis). Architectural considerations fed back to Greg; included in Sprint 3.

**2. Project Updates and Sprint 3 Focus**
Project status Green. Sprint 3 focuses: Change Management plan—impact analysis, Communications strategy and planning, Stakeholder Analysis; Knowledge Management—Service Desk loading knowledge in DMI, validation of Confluence integration for Knowledge; End User Portal—validating requirements against current state portal, writing stories for future development; CSDM/Service Modelling—focus on Application and Enterprise level.

**3. Future of Knowledge Management – Decision**
The Steering Committee agreed on a clear, standardised direction for Knowledge Management to support self-service and ServiceNow adoption. Knowledge will be standardised using existing ServiceNow knowledge templates already developed through FSD implementation. Internal Service Desk will be the first team to transition knowledge into ServiceNow. To facilitate increased end-user self-service and first contact resolution, teams should: convert articles to standardised templates; centralise end-user facing Knowledge Articles in ServiceNow DMI; increase number of Knowledge Articles by reviewing Level 2/3 Resolver articles to create more Knowledge Articles. **Decision agreed:** ServiceNow DMI and Confluence should be the primary knowledge platforms. End-user knowledge belongs in ServiceNow; other types may live in Confluence.

**4. Service Modelling Update**
Top Down Modelling: Enterprise Architect resources available from Darren Rowe’s team for the next 6 weeks for Top Down Modelling. Existing Business Capability Management is proving to map clearly to how CSDM needs to be set up. Bottom Up Modelling: Application Owners identified; kick-off meeting scheduled with identified Application owners for Tuesday 02/03 to provide overview of approach (Technical Services, Service Offerings, mapping apps to services), align on next steps and 2-week engagement plan to collect data and validate mappings. Shawn raised that service mapping is not a nice-to-have; the sooner service mapping is in place, the sooner the organisation gets real operational value. Project team agreed to keep Service Mapping top of mind as services are developed with owners over coming weeks.

**5. Zero Touch Service Desk**
Committee discussed approach to Zero Touch (self-service) Service Desk. Agreed useful to leverage existing analytics and reporting capability more actively to support Zero Touch initiatives. Power BI solution provides process analytics and insights for Service Desk analytics; can provide visibility into Service Desk contact channels (emails, phone, portal), Request types and resolution patterns to identify high-value automation and self-service opportunities. Next steps: Project team to develop clear definition and phased view of Zero Touch Service Desk for next steering committee.
