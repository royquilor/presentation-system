## 🎯 Context

A change management plan is a structured document that outlines how an organisation will manage changes to processes, systems, or personnel to ensure smooth transitions and minimise disruption. The Group IT Transition to ServiceNow uses a Change Management Plan spreadsheet and Change Toolkit to guide stakeholders through the transition. Key components include Change Description, Stakeholder Analysis, Communication Plan, and Training Strategy.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Organisational change without structured management leads to uncertainty, resistance, and disruption to operations and productivity. Stakeholders need clarity on what is changing and why, how they will be engaged, and what support and education they will receive. Without a roadmap, adoption suffers and benefits realisation is delayed.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Change Story**
Documents an agreed summary of the change by stakeholder groups. Results feed Change Impact Analysis, Communications Plan, and Training Plan.

**2. Stakeholder Analysis**
Identifies who is affected by the change and how to engage them. Goal: document agreed change summary by stakeholder groups. Results used for Change Impact Analysis, Communications, and Training Plan.

**3. Change Impact Analysis**
Identifies specific changes faced by stakeholders; categorised as people, process, or technology. Results feed Communication and Training Plan. Linked via spreadsheet.

**4. Communication**
Outlines how information will be shared with stakeholders to ensure transparency and buy-in. Part of the Change Management Plan spreadsheet.

**5. Training**
Details support and education needed to help people adapt to the change. Scoped from Change Impact Analysis and stakeholder needs.

**6. Champions and SMEs**
Capability-specific SMEs identified: Incident Management (Alexander Ponce Jnr, Jonathan Lim, Bruce Morrison); Problem Management (Raymond David, Marvin Lee, Jonathan Lim); Change Management (Lucy Ballaben, Patricia Adia); Request Management (Alexander Ponce Jnr, Jonathan Lim, Bruce Morrison); Service Operations Workspace (Jonathan Lim + Corporate Service Owners); Service Catalog (Alexander Ponce Jnr, Jonathan Lim, Patricia Adia, Bruce Morrison); Knowledge Management (Alexander Ponce Jnr, Jonathan Lim, Patricia Adia); Service Level Management (Jonathan Lim); Surveys (Sheryl Siew, Alexander Ponce Jnr, Patricia Adia); CMDB (Stan Hichens, Helen Vu, Minu Mathew, Gavin Millar).

**7. Plan artefacts**
Change Management Plan spreadsheet and Change Toolkit (PowerPoint) stored in SharePoint. Links provided for Stakeholder Analysis and Change Impact Analysis tabs.

**8. Benefits**
Reduces uncertainty by providing clarity and direction; increases adoption by involving and supporting stakeholders; minimises disruption to operations and productivity.

## 💡 Proposal

Execute the change management plan using the structured spreadsheet and Change Toolkit.

- Complete Stakeholder Analysis to document agreed change summary by stakeholder groups.
- Conduct Change Impact Analysis to identify people, process, and technology changes.
- Develop Communication Plan and Training Strategy from analysis results.
- Engage Champions and SMEs per capability area for validation, training, and adoption support.
- Use Change Toolkit for presentations and engagement activities.

*The plan provides a roadmap that guides the organisation through the transition, defining how changes will be communicated, executed, and reinforced for successful adoption and minimal resistance.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Risk:** Stakeholder engagement depends on timely completion of Change Story and Impact Analysis; delays cascade to Communications and Training.
- **Risk:** SME availability across 10 capability areas may constrain Champion engagement and training delivery.

---

## ✅ Next Steps

1. **Stakeholder Analysis** — Finalise agreed change summary by stakeholder groups; feed into Impact Analysis and Comms.
2. **Change Impact Analysis** — Complete people, process, and technology categorisation; feed into Training Plan.
3. **Communications** — Develop and execute Communication Plan based on analysis outputs.
4. **Training** — Scope and deliver training aligned to identified process changes and new roles.
5. **Champions** — Activate SME network for validation, training support, and adoption monitoring.
