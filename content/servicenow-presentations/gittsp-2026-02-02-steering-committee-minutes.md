## Context

Steering committee meeting for the Group IT Transition to ServiceNow project. Present: Shawn Sutton, Joe Thornley, Sumindre Gunawardane, Clinton Cooper, Julian Buckley, Jason Chau, Josh Jacobs, Jessie Gisler, Taumasina Keil. Absent: Jonathan Lim. The project entered a two-week sprint (2–13 February). The committee endorsed the indicative critical path and go-live timeline, and supported refinement of services modelling with enterprise architecture alignment.

---

## Problem

The committee needed to endorse the draft critical path and indicative delivery timeline for go-live. Key considerations included platform upgrades (April–May), security/BCP/compliance from Datacom-as-a-customer perspective, service modelling as an early dependency, integrations readiness, and avoiding overlap with GovNext April deployments. The committee also needed to align on services modelling approach and working group establishment (End User Portal, Knowledge Management).

---

## Observations

**1. Project Updates and Sprint Goals**
Jessie provided an update: team entering a two-week sprint (2–13 February). Sprint goals: Discovery Workshops with Corporate and EAS Teams (week 2) to capture current state people, process, tools; End User Portal Working Group establishment with initial delivery requirements and timeline; Knowledge Management Working Group establishment with initial delivery requirements and timeline for DMI Knowledge Base structure cleansing and transition; Architecture of DMI Use Cases / Reference Architecture Document drafted for next Steer Co; advance Services Modelling with data ahead of next Sprint’s session with Owners. Project continues in alignment with ITOM Discovery and AIOps/Logic Monitor; session scheduled Tuesday 3 Feb with Julian Buckley, Steve Winton, Matt Shepheard. Resource update: Taumasina Keil as Project Coordinator; Greg Burns as Platform Architect (commitment of time); Sandra Mendes with increased availability following large website project closing.

**2. Critical Path and Indicative Delivery Timeline**
The draft timeline was presented. Key points: ServiceNow upgrades expected around April–May, dates not yet confirmed; preferred to complete upgrades before go-live but not viewed as hard critical path. Security, BCP and compliance (SS): explicitly account from Datacom-as-a-customer perspective, address during delivery rather than post go-live. Service modelling dependency: key early dependency driving assignment groups, knowledge ownership, catalogue items, CIs, surveys, integrations; rework later would materially impact timeline. Integrations readiness: clearer current-state view being worked through; Jessie to circulate. Mobile app: not critical path but potential go-live value add if enabled by May. Internal vs customer go-live (JC): internal go-live does not have to align exactly with customer go-live; can be used as risk-management lever. Go-live timing (group): strong support for go-live in May to avoid overlap with GovNext April deployments and reduce pressure on shared resources. The indicative timeline was endorsed and can now be socialised with AiOps and FSD Programme Team, then developed in more detail to show technical delivery steps and post go-live support resource requirements.

**3. Services Modelling**
Josh Jacobs provided verbal update on modelling work and preparation for meetings with Service Owners (Sprint 2). Approach validated by Glenn Guzzo. Committee supported refinement of Services Modelling for next sprint (16–27 Feb). Additional considerations: enterprise architecture alignment—involving EA was suggested to ensure service models align across ServiceNow, security, and architecture views, reducing rework risk; Jessie to action EA inclusion. Ensure ongoing resilience and security considerations built into Services. Next steps to return to Steering Committee 16/02.

**4. AOB – Automation, End User Portal, Benefit Realisation, Mobile App**
Automation opportunities should be considered alongside effort to build and maintain service catalogue items underpinning workflows, including existing apps (e.g. zScaler) and new technologies. End User Portal noted as transformational opportunity aligning with FSD work stream. Benefit Realisation: FSD refreshing ServiceNow-associated Benefits; Shawn encouraged leaders to consider actual realisation of benefits and what is being done with that value gain. Mobile App: project team to investigate mobile app setup, assuming OOTB use.
