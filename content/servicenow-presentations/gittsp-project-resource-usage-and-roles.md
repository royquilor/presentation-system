# Project Resource Usage and Roles
> Roles, responsibilities, and resource allocation for Group IT Transition to ServiceNow

**Author:** Muskan Verma
**Date:** 10 February 2026

## 🎯 Context

The Group IT Transition to ServiceNow project relies on a defined set of roles with clear responsibilities and FTE allocations. Resource planning is tracked via a High-Level Resource Planning spreadsheet. Approved FTE allocations run until May 2026 for key roles. Timesheeting uses DATACOM INTERNAL K Code with Internal Reference Note "Internal Transition Project" until a project k-code is assigned.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Without clear role definitions and resource allocation, the project risks overlap, gaps, and misalignment between delivery teams. Architectural decisions, discovery workshops, and change management require dedicated capacity. Platform build, testing, and OCM need coordinated effort across internal and external team members.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Platform Architect (Greg Burns, 0.25 FTE)**
Immediate needs: Service-Bridge Architecture Blueprint (source/target systems, data flows, security, domain separation); Leveraged Team Use Case and Technical Design (MSP + DMI workspace for internal-only vs dual-role users); Reporting Architecture (operational and commercial reporting separation with management consolidation). Ensures technical integrity, DMI fit for purpose, tenancy rationalisation, integration planning, RBAC/ACL, DR Plan, and embedded test processes.

**2. Senior Business Analyst (Sandra Mendes, 0.5 FTE)**
Approved until May 2026. Facilitates Discovery Workshops; elicits and documents business objectives; validates use cases with Architect; leads requirements elicitation and validation; develops As-Is and To-Be process models; creates Requirements Traceability Matrix; supports solution verification and product acceptance.

**3. Datacom Project Manager (Jessie Gisler, 0.75 FTE)**
Approved until May 2026. Plans, monitors, and controls delivery; maintains Project Plan; manages risks, issues, dependencies, changes; reports to Steering Committee and FSD Programme; leads resources; coordinates Platform Delivery and ITOM Discovery; oversees AIOps/LogicMonitor rollout; ensures testing and business acceptance.

**4. Discovery Lead (Jonathan Lim, 0.25 FTE)**
Approved until May 2026. SME and go-to contact; advises on technical, process, and operational requirements; validates product designs; contributes to risk identification; participates in quality reviews; documents MIM and DR processes.

**5. Discovery Back-up (Patricia Adia, 0.1 FTE, approved 0.25 FTE)**
Key SME; validates designs; coordinates action items and cross-team work; acts as Transition Manager with Corporate Resolver groups.

**6. Organisation Change Management (Muskan Verma, 0.5 FTE)**
Approved until May 2026. Develops Change Management Strategy and Plan; communication strategy and plan; identifies change impacts, stakeholders, readiness; prepares communications, training, engagement; manages resistance; monitors adoption and benefits post-implementation.

**7. Project Coordination (Sophia Black, Taumasina Keil)**
Taumasina: meeting notes, Jira/Confluence maintenance, departmental coordination, communication support. Sophia: planning, scheduling, coordination; platform team perspective; onboarding and access; internal processes and standards; platform and technical coordination; documentation.

**8. ServiceNow Platform Team Admin ITSM (Peter Song)**
Owns ITSM modules (forms, workflows, automation, configuration); builds users, groups, roles, core platform elements; collaborates with architects on CMDB design; supports build testing and platform stability; establishes operational processes for change, incident handling, maintenance.

## 💡 Proposal

Maintain role clarity and resource allocation through the High-Level Resource Planning spreadsheet and regular status updates.

- Ensure Platform Architect delivers Service-Bridge Blueprint, Leveraged Team Use Case, and Reporting Architecture in next 4 weeks.
- Support Discovery Lead and Back-up with workshop facilitation and SME validation.
- Coordinate Project Manager, OCM, and Project Coordination for aligned delivery and change management.
- Use DATACOM INTERNAL K Code with "Internal Transition Project" for timesheeting until k-code assigned.

*Clear roles and FTE allocations until May 2026 enable focused delivery across architecture, discovery, build, and change management.*
