# S1. Knowledge Management
> Knowledge Management capability for DMI internal services

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 18 February 2026
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40758738996

---

## 🎯 Context

This document defines the S1 Knowledge Management scope for the DMI instance. The capability will be fully configured for Datacom internal services, with categorised and tagged articles consolidated from existing sources, approval and review workflows, user access defined, and handover to BAU complete. Linked to Jira CGIDTTS-112.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Knowledge is currently scattered across seven channels (Cherwell, Confluence, Unity, Team Sharepoints, Teams Links, OneNote, ServiceNow MSP). This results in low governance, ownership, and visibility. End-user self-service and first contact resolution require a centralised, governed knowledge base.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Current knowledge article types and locations**
Self Service/Level 1: Unity, SharePoint, Cherwell. Level 2/3 Technical: Cherwell, Confluence, Unity, Team Sharepoints, Teams Links, OneNote. Leveraged Teams Level 2/3: ServiceNow MSP, Confluence, Offline.

**2. Future state—Knowledge base**
Central repository accessible by all users through Employee Centre Portal. Article types: User Guide, How To. Location: ServiceNow DMI.

**3. Future state—IT base**
Central repository for all IT Community Articles (except Known Errors). Restricted to Fulfillers. Article types: Product guides, Policy, Procedural Documentation. Location: ServiceNow DMI and/or Confluence (with appropriate tagging).

**4. Future state—Known Error base**
Central repository for all IT-facing Known Error articles. Restricted to Fulfillers. Article types: Known Error articles. Location: ServiceNow DMI and/or Confluence (with appropriate tagging).

**5. Discovery workshop finding**
Seven different channels/repositories identified: Cherwell, Confluence, Unity, Team Sharepoints, Teams Links, OneNote, ServiceNow MSP. Low governance, ownership, visibility.

**6. Proposed standardisation**
Convert articles to standardised templates; move end-user facing "Knowledge Articles" to ServiceNow DMI; increase Knowledge Articles by reviewing Level 2/3 Resolver articles to create more end-user content.

**7. Decision required**
Steering Committee 2 March. Seeking approval to instruct knowledge to be stored and managed either in ServiceNow DMI or Confluence, with drive to increase Knowledge Articles available for end users in DMI.

**8. Supporting resources**
Datacom Knowledge Business Process, FSD Knowledge Management documents, KM Framework and ServiceNow Basics Overview, Datacademy Training for Knowledge Management.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Standardise Knowledge Management by consolidating into ServiceNow DMI (and Confluence where appropriate). Increase end-user facing Knowledge Articles to support self-service and first contact resolution.

- Convert articles to standardised templates
- Move end-user Knowledge Articles to ServiceNow DMI
- Review Level 2/3 Resolver articles to create more Knowledge Articles
- Obtain Steering Committee approval for DMI vs Confluence storage decision

*Centralised knowledge with governance enables self-service and reduces L1 load.*
