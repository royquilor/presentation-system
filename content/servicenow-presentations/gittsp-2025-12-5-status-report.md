A pause was placed on engaging with stakeholders on "Current State" discovery to make space for the Service Led Discovery approach. Discovery work has been largely data-driven; the team will slowly continue to clean up and validate for a base impacted User / Stakeholder register.

**2. Internal Service Desk Progress**
Jonathan's team are making great progress refreshing and reviewing Cherwell to Service touchpoints for migration. Examples: Review Knowledge Articles (register of articles, review dates, Cherwell groups/reference numbers); Survey analysis (validating Survey Manager types replaceable by ServiceNow Surveys); Notification analysis (validating notifications sent today).

**3. FSD GROWTH Stream**
FSD GROWTH Stream—with ServiceNow and additional Development resources—has SN to SN as a primary initiative to deliver by March 2026. Datacom (with DMI Instance) are a Customer of the MSP instance, making them eligible as first Customer for Service Bridge Implementation.

**4. Datacom SN to SN Development Approved**
On Wed 3/12 Peter Lawther approved Datacom's SN to SN Development. This is a critical dependency for DMI Go-Live and Datacom transition. Jessie will represent the project in the GROWTH Stream, with Clinton Lees / Stephen Hille as Business SMEs.

**5. ITOM Session**
Session happening on Tuesday to regroup internally and compare Azure and AWS and ITOM functionality. The team will review the ServiceNow option paper and document use cases for Datacom DMI (and future customers) for Steer-co agreement early next year.

**6. Architecture Use Case Analysis**
Queries have gone out to Senior Team members of the variety of Cherwell Tenancies and Group data to query whether fields are used in any reporting they oversee. A register of "current use cases" will be presented early 2026.
