# Zero Touch Service Desk
> Always-on, self-service-first service experience for Datacom Corporate users

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 26 February 2026
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40822964286

---

## 🎯 Context

Datacom Corporate aims to create an always-on, self-service-first service experience where 80%+ of interactions are fulfilled automatically without human intervention. The initiative leverages ServiceNow's DMI instance, Generative AI, Virtual Agent, predictive workflows, and proactive monitoring to demonstrate the showcase customer model for FSD adoption across Datacom.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Traditional service desk operations rely on manual ticket creation, queues, and waiting time for common issues. Human analysts spend time on repetitive, low-value work instead of complex, high-value activities. The current model does not scale efficiently or deliver the modern, automated experience users expect.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Portal-first logging**
Users go to the DMI Employee Centre for requests and incidents. The "How can I help today" portal box enables knowledge, Confluence, and catalogue item search before ticket creation.

**2. Self-service by default**
Knowledge, AI Search, and pre-validated workflows resolve issues at first contact. Internal KAs and Confluence integrated search will be in DMI UAT for rollout to Prod at go-live.

**3. Virtual Agent (Now Assist)**
Conversational interface in portal and mobile powers catalog conversations, simple request fulfilment, and guided troubleshooting across Corporate EAS catalog.

**4. GenAI and AI Search**
Contextual Q&A across Corporate knowledge base, auto-summarisation of interactions, and next-best-action suggestions deflect tickets before logging.

**5. Proactive monitoring (ITOM + LM)**
MID Server deployment for Corporate device/service discovery, automated remediation scripts linked to events, and user notifications post-fix. Human analysts handle exceptions only.

**6. Phased delivery**
Baseline and Foundations (Channel Establishment) scheduled 13 Mar 2026; Service Request and Catalogue Items on portal targeted 17 Apr 2026. Admin-heavy work prioritised as quick wins at go-live with enhancements roadmapped.

**7. Operating model alignment**
Automation-first intake, routing by automation before humans, feedback loops for KB and runbooks. Phone and email limited to emergencies or escalations.

**8. Knowledge management**
Corporate KB curated for self-service, auto-generation from resolved tickets, and inline surfacing in Virtual Agent and AI Search results.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Establish a zero-touch service desk through phased implementation of portal-first channels, self-service catalogue items, and automation.

- Deploy portal-first logging with integrated knowledge and Confluence search by 13 Mar 2026.
- Identify quick-win catalogue items and service requests for go-live; roadmap enhancements for post-transition.
- Review and reset existing KAs as "User facing" or "Technical team" artefacts to maximise self-serve resolution.
- Configure auto-routing rules to automation handlers and embed KB search before ticket logging.
- Establish portal content governance and marketing of Portal First approach.

*The goal is to eliminate queues and waiting time for common issues while freeing analysts for complex work and demonstrating FSD adoption success.*
