Steering committee meeting for the Group IT Transition to ServiceNow project. ---

## Problem

The team needed to pivot from data-driven discovery to a service-led approach. Future State Mapping remained not possible; FSD advised that future/current state process maps do not exist and the High Level solution design would be used as best fit. The committee needed to align on defining the services model, prioritising top services for the taxonomy, and addressing ITOM visibility, CMDB, and licensing considerations.

---

## Observations

**1. General Update and Status**
A pause was placed on engaging stakeholders on Current State discovery to make space for the Service Led Discovery approach. Discovery work had been largely data-driven; the team would slowly continue to clean up and validate to have a base impacted User/Stakeholder register and resume discussion in the New Year. Future State Mapping follow-up: FSD advised future/current state process maps do not exist; High Level solution design will be used as best fit.

**2. Service Led Discovery Approach**
Following Gartner’s recommendation, the remainder of the year would focus on defining the Services Model. A workshop was scheduled where participants would: review the full list of approximately 68 known internal services; categorise them; prioritise the top 12 services to become the foundation of Datacom’s service taxonomy. A working spreadsheet with all known services was prepared and shared. Purpose: create a model that scales and avoids premature detailed discovery; align service modelling to CSDM and ServiceNow best practice; leverage Gartner examples and frameworks. Examples (e.g. Network Services) may be pre-mapped to help the team understand patterns. Importance placed on understanding what constitutes a service (e.g. VPN, CRM, laptops) vs tools or integrations.

**3. ITOM Visibility, CSDM, CMDB, and Discovery Challenges**
Updates on internal CMDB discovery: asset discovery largely completed; relationship mapping needs refinement; clarification around what the ACC agent can and cannot discover (e.g. server-to-server relationships require horizontal discovery).

**4. Licensing Considerations (Visibility, Service Graph Connectors)**
Visibility licensing is required when using Service Graph Connectors. This applies to corporate and customer environments if they leverage those integrations. Datacom has included licensing costs in the AI Ops uplift. The team needs a sustainable cost allocation approach for licences across customers.

---

## Proposal

- Adopt Service-Led Discovery approach; define services model before resuming detailed stakeholder discovery.
- Workshop to categorise and prioritise top 12 services from approximately 68 known services.
- Jessie to circulate proposed Services model as a starter ahead of workshop.
- Address ITOM licensing and cost allocation for Visibility/Service Graph Connectors.

---

## Risks

- **CMDB relationship mapping:** Refinement needed; ACC agent limitations for server-to-server relationships.
- **Licensing cost allocation:** Sustainable approach required for Visibility licensing across corporate and customer environments.
