**3. Scope & Team**
Focus on internal Datacom corporate and associated areas. Not customer-facing MSP instance groups or teams.

**4. Governance & Reporting**
Fortnightly steering committee meetings proposed. Status reports and resource tracking to be sent out regularly. Dependencies: FSD program and ServiceNow professional services.

**5. Project Identity**
