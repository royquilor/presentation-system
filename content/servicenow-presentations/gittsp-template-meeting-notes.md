## 🎯 Context

This is the Meeting Notes template for the Group IT Transition to ServiceNow (GITTSP) project. It provides a standard structure for documenting meetings: Date, Attendance (Present/Absent), Discussion topics, Action items, Decisions, and Related info. Core project members include Jessie Gisler, Shawn Sutton, Joe Thornley, Jonathan Lim, Sumindre Gunawardane, Joshua Jacobs, Clinton Cooper, and Jason Chau.
