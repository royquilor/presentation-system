## 🎯 Context

This document defines the FSD (Future State Delivery) product and process owners. It provides a single reference for accountability across ITSM, CSM, ITOM, and CMDB products and their associated processes.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Clear ownership is essential for design decisions, process alignment, and governance. Without defined product and process owners, decisions can be delayed or inconsistent across the ServiceNow implementation.

## 💡 Proposal

Use the FSD People and Process Owners as the authoritative reference for design decisions and process alignment. Engage Process Owners for targeted deep dives and discovery workshops.

- Engage Process Owners for process-specific discovery sessions
- Resolve Discovery ownership transition (Modern Ops customer leads or TOC)
- Ensure Product Owners are consulted for product-level decisions

*Clear ownership enables timely decisions and consistent process design.*
