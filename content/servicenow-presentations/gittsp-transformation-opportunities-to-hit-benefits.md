## 🎯 Context

This document maps transformation opportunities to the benefits they enable. Each opportunity describes the future state (6+ months) and the impact on operational metrics, enabling the programme to track value realisation.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Transformation programmes must demonstrate value. Mapping opportunities to benefits ensures alignment between delivery and business outcomes, and provides a framework for measuring success.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Portal usage**
Transformation: Shift from email logging to portal logging across all internal domains. Inherent improvement—enables automation, categorisation, downstream efficiencies. Impact: reduced ticket volumes, improved time to resolution. Future: integrate Virtual Agent, standardise inputs.

**2. CMDB and assets**
Transformation: Manage CMDB/assets with ITOM Discovery. Align Datacom CMDB, single source of truth, auto-routing, relationship mapping, formal ownership. Impact: streamlined reporting, accuracy in asset lifecycle, enable event management. Future: Service Mapping.

**3. Incidents, requests, problems, maintenance**
Transformation: Inherent—portal submission, CI-based auto-assignment; known errors/workarounds capture; standardise requests; suggest Knowledge Articles. Impact: lower mean time to resolution, fewer repeats. Future: trend identification, maintenance reduction.

**4. Monitor and respond to events**
Transformation: Root cause analysis, dependency maps, CI clustering. Impact: faster resolution, less downtime. Future: integrate with incident/problem workflow for rapid RCA.

**5. Knowledge and catalogue items**
Transformation: Inherent—improved feedback loop, auto-review schedules, portal entry standardisation; single KB location; link KAs to catalogue; redefine offerings. Impact: better self-help adoption, reduced L1 load. Future: integrate with Confluence.

**6. Benefit themes**
Operational efficiency (reduced ticket volumes, FTE hours); faster resolution (MTTR, less downtime); accuracy and governance (CMDB, asset lifecycle); self-service (knowledge, portal); automation (routing, event management).

**7. Dependencies**
Portal usage enables downstream automation and categorisation. CMDB/Discovery enables auto-routing and event management. Knowledge and catalogue enable self-help and deflection.

**8. Future state (6+ months)**
Virtual Agent integration; Service Mapping; trend identification and maintenance reduction; incident/problem workflow integration for RCA; Confluence integration for knowledge.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Execute transformation opportunities in sequence to maximise benefit realisation. Portal and CMDB are foundational; incidents, events, and knowledge build on these.

- Prioritise portal adoption and CMDB/Discovery for foundational benefits
- Implement incident, request, problem improvements with CI-based auto-assignment
- Enable event monitoring and response with root cause analysis
- Consolidate knowledge and catalogue for self-help adoption

*Transformation opportunities map directly to reduced costs, faster resolution, and improved self-service.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Sequencing:** Benefits depend on foundational capabilities (portal, CMDB)—delays cascade.
- **Adoption:** Portal and knowledge benefits require user adoption; change management critical.

---

## ✅ Next Steps

1. **Benefit tracking** — Establish KPIs for ticket volumes, MTTR, self-help adoption, CMDB accuracy.
2. **Opportunity sequencing** — Confirm delivery sequence aligns with benefit dependencies.
3. **Future state planning** — Plan Virtual Agent, Service Mapping, Confluence integration for 6+ month horizon.
