## Context

Steering committee meeting for the Group IT Transition to ServiceNow project. Present: Shawn Sutton, Sumindre Gunawardane, Clinton Cooper, Julian Buckley, Jason Chau, Josh Jacobs, Jessie Gisler, Jonathan Lim, Melissa Compologno. Absent: Joe Thornley. ## Problem

Emerging risks included Datacom alignment on MSP/DMI use cases creating confusion for the FSD Programme, and Future State Mapping being blocked (FSD advised post-meeting that future/current state process maps do not exist; the High Level solution design would be used as best fit). The committee needed to approve the detailed discovery plan, understand ITOM progress, and address immediate resource gaps including a ServiceNow solution architect.

---

## Observations

**1. General Update and Emerging Risks**
Initiation phase exited; project now in Discovery. Jira refresh of Epic>Story>Subtask completed; Status Report would be accurate from end of week. Key risks: Datacom alignment on MSP/DMI use cases creating confusion (e.g. Datapay directed to this project then interviewed by FSD INWOW Transformation); Jessie to get use cases documented for analysis. Future State Mapping was blocked; Jessie and Jason to follow up. Post-meeting: FSD advised future/current state process maps do not exist; High Level solution design will be used as best fit.

**2. Detailed Discovery Plan**
The team completed two weeks of data discovery, analysing users, resolver teams, integrations, CMDB, knowledge articles, notifications, and change management processes. Next phase: hour-long, in-person validation sessions with resolver group team leads, targeting all relevant Group IT managers. Sessions use targeted questions to confirm use cases and validate data findings. Discovery sessions avoid duplicating effort and group teams that work closely together; they also serve as OCM touchpoints. Plan to complete all discovery sessions before mid-December (many on leave after 15th); remaining activities focus on process mapping close-out and finalising transformation opportunities.

**3. ITOM Discovery High-Level Update**
Internal rollout of ServiceNow Item Discovery underway; mid server deployment design about 90% complete. Item Discovery deployed on servers in test environment; validation ongoing to resolve discrepancies in CI counts. Network and cloud (Azure, AWS) discovery in progress; service graph connectors being tested. Internal rollout serves as a pilot to gather learnings before customer deployment. Further detail to be provided at next meeting.

**4. Project Resource Model and Forecast**
Jessie presented a resource model mapping team members to phases: discovery, delivery, implementation, training, transition. Key contributors: Jessie and Jonathan (discovery lead), Patricia as SME. Muskan as OCM at 0.5 FTE through April. Additional support requested from Devon (BA) and Sandra (senior BA/team lead) for scoping and transformational opportunities. Resource plan anticipates clarity after discovery; training and transition toward end of March. Melissa Compologno queried whether FSD OCM work could be leveraged; Jessie to align with Jason on what exists to avoid duplication.

**5. Immediate Resource Gaps**
Jessie identified a gap for a ServiceNow solution architect (or similar) to address uncertainties around data flows, domain separation, reporting, and tenancy models for Datacom as a customer. Need estimated at 0.5 FTE for about three weeks, adjustable. Team will gather use cases from business units (DCS, SAS, PSG) to inform requirements and consult experts as needed.
