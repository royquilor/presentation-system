# DRAFT HL Business Case
> High-Level Business Case – ServiceNow Implementation for Datacom Internal IT

**Author:** Jessie Gisler
**Date:** 27 January 2026
**Source:** https://datacomgroup.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GITTSP/pages/40343699766

---

## 🎯 Context

Datacom's internal IT service desk of approximately 20 agents services roughly 6,500 staff across Australia and New Zealand, supported by multiple resolver groups. Current operations rely heavily on email-driven ticket logging, legacy workflows, and inconsistent configuration management data. Implementing ServiceNow will deliver a modern, self-service, workflow-enabled environment underpinned by a clean, governed CMDB.

---

## 🔍 Problem

Constraints from email-driven logging, legacy workflows, and inconsistent CMDB data limit the ability to automate workflows, standardise processes, and gain actionable insights. These limitations reduce productivity, extend resolution times, and prevent alignment with Datacom's broader ServiceNow adoption strategy.

---

## 📋 Observations

**1. Strategic drivers**
Modernise service interaction by shifting from email to portal-based ticketing; enable self-help and automation to reduce Level 1 load through virtual agents and embedded knowledge; establish a governed CMDB as single source of truth; improve ITIL process maturity with automated categorisation, assignment, and proactive problem management; align internal IT with enterprise ServiceNow strategy.

**2. Expected efficiency gains**
15–30% reduction in service desk ticket volumes via self-service and virtual agent; 20–40% reduction in Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) through automated categorisation and routing.

**3. Process maturity targets**
Increased proportion of standard changes (>50%) lowering operational risk; accurate CMDB driving automation in incident, request, and change processes.

**4. Capability improvements**
Portal usage shifts from email to direct portal logging with self-help virtual agent; CMDB establishes AU/NZ single source of truth with auto-routing; Incident Management enables portal submission with automated categorisation and CI-based assignment; Request Fulfilment provides portal-enabled standardised requests with auto assignment.

**5. Implementation scope**
Internal IT service desk (20 staff) plus associated resolver groups; approximately 6,500 end users across Australia and New Zealand.

**6. Timeline and dependencies**
4–6 months from initiation to go-live with phased rollout of portal, CMDB, and automation; dependencies include resolver group engagement for CMDB ownership, change management for staff adoption, and alignment with enterprise ServiceNow governance.

**7. User experience and operational insight**
Improved satisfaction scores via faster responses and self-help; transparent tracking for end users; data-driven reporting with trend identification; informed capacity planning and proactive improvement initiatives.

**8. Asset and event management**
Assets managed separately from CIs with clear lifecycle tracking; ITOM enables automated monitoring and dependency-based root cause analysis; Service Mapping to visualise dependencies and link to CI clusters.

---

## 💡 Proposal

Implement ServiceNow for Datacom internal IT to deliver a modern, self-service, workflow-enabled environment with a governed CMDB.

- Shift from email to portal-based ticketing with Virtual Agent and AI Search
- Establish governed CMDB with auto-routing for incidents and requests
- Implement ITIL-aligned incident, request, change, problem, and knowledge processes
- Deploy phased rollout over 4–6 months (portal, CMDB, automation)
- Engage resolver groups for CMDB ownership and change management

*ServiceNow adoption will increase productivity, reduce resolution times, improve user experience, and support Datacom's strategic alignment with customers and market expectations.*

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- **Resolver group engagement:** CMDB ownership and data cleansing require sustained engagement from resolver groups; delays may impact automation benefits.
- **Change adoption:** Staff adoption of portal-first and new workflows depends on effective change management and training.

---

## ✅ Next Steps

1. **Validate scope and timeline** — Confirm 4–6 month implementation window with resolver group availability.
2. **Establish CMDB ownership** — Define data ownership and cleansing requirements with resolver groups.
3. **Align with enterprise governance** — Ensure implementation follows FSD and ServiceNow platform governance.

---

## 🔑 Close

> Implementing ServiceNow for internal IT will deliver a modern, self-service, workflow-enabled environment, underpinned by a clean, governed CMDB—increasing productivity, reducing resolution times, and improving user experiences.
