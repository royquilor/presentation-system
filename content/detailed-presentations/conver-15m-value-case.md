## 🎯 Context

Conver is Datacom's enterprise AI platform — providing 3,000+ employees access to 13 AI models through a single secure interface. Built by a team of 9, it shipped 14 features to production and generates a conservatively estimated $15.6M NZD in annual productivity value for under $1.7M total investment. ## 🔍 Problem

Datacom's only alternatives were Microsoft Copilot at $50/user/month ($3.9M/year for 6,500 staff) or accepting the security risk of ungoverned shadow AI. There was no way to give every employee access to GPT-4o, Claude, and Gemini in a secure, audited environment — until the Insights & Analytics team built one.

## 📋 Observations

**1. $15.6M annual value — the conservative estimate**
6,500 employees saving just 1 hour per week at $50/hr loaded cost, across 48 working weeks = $15.6M NZD. The moderate scenario (3 hrs/week) reaches $46.8M. The optimistic scenario (5 hrs/week) reaches $78M. Even the floor delivers 826% ROI.

**2. Break-even requires saving just 5 minutes a day**
The tool only needs to save 0.1% of an employee's working time to justify itself. At $10/user/month variable cost, the productivity gain from one employee saving $10,000/month covers 1,000 users. With 30,000+ conversations already, employees are demonstrably using AI every day.

**3. 99% cheaper than Microsoft Copilot**
Conver: ~$5/user/month. Copilot: $50/user/month. For 6,500 users that's $390K/year vs $3.9M/year. Conver also provides 13 models across 4 providers while Copilot locks into one ecosystem — and Conver can be commercialised externally.

**4. 600% year-over-year adoption growth**
2024 Nov-Dec: 1,217 conversations. 2025 Q1: 3,700. 2025 Q2: 10,090. 2025 Q3 (8 days): 1,455. Total: 16,462+ conversations. 54 power users, 73 core adopters. This is organic adoption — no mandate, no forced training. People choose to use it.

**5. 14 features shipped by a team of 9**
Multi-model chat, Code Interpreter, Memories, Autodocx v1.2, Knowledge Base, Assistants Menu, Agent Handoffs, Feature Flags, Zendesk SITC Integration, SPP/OpenAir, Policy Chat Bot, Brand Agent, Bing Search, and Conver Agents. That's 1.6 features per person. This team is an AI capability factory.

**6. 10 more features in the pipeline**
SharePoint integration, Admin Panel, Agent Marketplace, and 115+ DataScape agents are planned. The platform architecture (serverless on Azure) is designed to scale. Every new feature compounds the productivity value for 6,500 users.

**7. Total investment: $905K–$1.685M**
Azure hosting: $180K–$360K. AI tokens (charged back to business units): $375K–$625K. Staff (4.0 FTE): $350K–$700K. Infrastructure alone costs just $35K/year for 6,500 users. This is a live, serving platform — not speculative R&D.

**8. Hiring avoidance compounds the value**
Phase 1 targets IT Support, HR, and Software Development. If Conver enables teams to absorb 10% workload growth without hiring, and each avoided hire costs $100K loaded, just 10 avoided hires saves $1M/year — from a platform that already exists.

**9. 13 AI models across 4 providers — best-in-class choice**
GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, o1, o3-mini, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3 Opus, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Gemini 2.0 Flash, DALL-E 3, Whisper, and more. Users get the right model for the right task. No single-vendor lock-in.
